What you can do

A place to remember every book.

Build your shelf

Add books manually or search Open Library when connected. Organize by year and reading status; keep private notes and ratings.

Keep going offline

After your first visit, once the app files are available on your device, you can browse and edit your shelf, add books manually, and make a JSON backup without a connection.

Take a copy with you

Export and restore a JSON file on your own device. The file contains private data, so keep it somewhere you trust. Installing the app does not create a backup.

Open to explore and contribute

Free and MIT licensed.

The source code is public. Read the MIT license, explore the repository, and follow the contribution guide.

Your library is local. The app does not receive your books, notes, ratings, or backups. See the privacy page for optional choices and external services.
